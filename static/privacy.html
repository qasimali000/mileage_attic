Privacy

← The Mileage Attic

The Mileage Attic turns mileage notes into a claim-ready CSV.

What we store

  • Your name, email address and phone number, when you first sign in, plus when you last signed in and how many times.
  • A record of each calculation: your email, the time, the number of journeys and total miles. We do not keep your notes, postcodes, name or the generated CSV.
  • Postcode-to-postcode distances, which are not linked to you, are cached to make the app faster.

Why

To count how many people use the app and to contact you about it if needed. We do not sell or share your name, email or phone number.

Third parties

Postcodes are sent to postcodes.io and OpenRouteService to work out distances. The app is hosted on Railway.

Your data

To have your email and records deleted, contact the app owner and we will remove them.
